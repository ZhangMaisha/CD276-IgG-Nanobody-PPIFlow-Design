
Interpretation guide:

RMSD:
A stable complex should show an initial rise followed by a plateau. Continuous increase suggests structural drift.

Rg:
Stable Rg indicates that the complex remains compact. Continuous increase suggests loosening or unfolding.

Interface contacts:
Stable antigen-binder association should maintain interface contacts. A drop toward zero suggests dissociation.

Hydrogen bonds:
H-bonds can fluctuate, but persistent or recurrent interfacial H-bonds support interface stability.

Minimum distance:
Stable complexes should maintain low binder-antigen minimum distance. Sudden increase suggests separation.

RMSF:
CDR loops are expected to be more flexible than framework regions. Excessive RMSF in CDRs together with contact/hbond loss suggests unstable binding.

SASA:
Stable SASA suggests no major unfolding or exposure change. A large continuous increase may indicate structural loosening.

Temperature, pressure, density:
These are primarily MD quality-control metrics. Temperature should remain near 300 K and density should be stable.
